# Problem 04

## The original problem

For

$$y^2=12x,$$

identify the vertex, axis of symmetry, and opening direction. Find points on the parabola for $x=3$ and use them to make a sketch.

## What we are looking for

We need the vertex, axis, opening direction, and two points with $x=3$.

## The idea we need

A parabola with equation $y^2=4px$ has vertex $(0,0)$, axis the $x$-axis, and opens right if $p>0$. The parameter satisfies $4p=12$.

## Step-by-step solution

Match $y^2=12x$ with $y^2=4px$: $4p=12$, so $p=3$.

Vertex: $(0,0)$. Axis of symmetry: the $x$-axis ($y=0$). Opening direction: to the right, since $p>0$.

For $x=3$: $y^2=12\cdot3=36$, so $y=\pm6$. Points: $(3,6)$ and $(3,-6)$.

## Why each step is valid

The standard parabola $y^2=4px$ gives the vertex at the origin and focus at $(p,0)$. Solving for $y$ at a given $x$ gives the symmetric points about the axis.

## Final answer (in words)

The parabola has vertex $(0,0)$, axis the $x$-axis, and opens to the right. For $x=3$ the points are $(3,6)$ and $(3,-6)$.

## Verification

Check $(3,6)$: $6^2=36$ and $12\cdot3=36$. Check $(3,-6)$: $(-6)^2=36$. Both satisfy the equation.

## Common mistakes

- Treating $12$ as $p$ instead of $4p$.
- Saying the parabola opens left.
- Forgetting the symmetric point below the axis.

## How I would explain it in class

I match the equation to $y^2=4px$ to find $p$. The vertex is the origin and the axis is the $x$-axis. For $x=3$ I get two symmetric points that help sketch the curve.
